Read the following passage.

A. Renewable energy sources, such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power, are becoming increasingly important in the global effort to reduce carbon emissions and combat climate change. Unlike fossil fuels, renewable energy sources do not produce greenhouse gases or other pollutants during operation. This makes them a cleaner alternative for generating electricity and powering industries.

B. Solar energy harnesses the power of the sun through photovoltaic cells, which convert sunlight directly into electricity. These cells are typically installed on rooftops or in large solar farms. Wind energy, on the other hand, is generated by wind turbines that convert the kinetic energy of wind into mechanical power. Hydroelectric power is produced by capturing the energy of flowing water, usually through dams built on large rivers.

C. The adoption of renewable energy sources has several economic benefits. It creates jobs in manufacturing, installation, and maintenance of renewable energy infrastructure. Additionally, it reduces dependence on imported fuels, enhancing energy security. Governments around the world are investing in renewable energy projects and offering incentives to encourage both businesses and households to switch to cleaner energy sources.

D. Despite their many advantages, renewable energy sources also face challenges. Solar and wind power generation can be intermittent, as they depend on weather conditions. This variability requires advancements in energy storage technologies to ensure a stable supply of electricity. Furthermore, the initial costs of setting up renewable energy infrastructure can be high, although they are decreasing with technological advancements and economies of scale.

E. Not given in any of the above paragraphs.

Decide which paragraph, A to D, has the information given in each statement below. Select E if the information is not given in any of the paragraphs.

-1. Renewable energy sources help in reducing carbon emissions.
-2. Solar energy is generated using photovoltaic cells.
-3. Renewable energy can create job opportunities.
-4. Wind turbines convert wind into mechanical power.
-5. Governments are offering incentives to promote renewable energy.
-6. One of the challenges of renewable energy is its dependence on weather conditions.
-7. Initial costs of renewable energy infrastructure are a concern.
-8. Solar farms are often located in remote areas to maximize sunlight exposure.
-9. Hydroelectric power utilizes the flow of water to generate electricity.
