Read the following passage.

A. The vaquita is a small porpoise species native exclusively to the northern part of the Gulf of California, Mexico. Recognized by its dark rings around the eyes and lips, the vaquita is the smallest and most endangered marine mammal in the world. Estimates in recent years suggest that fewer than 20 individuals remain, placing the vaquita on the brink of extinction.

B. The primary threat to the vaquita's survival is illegal fishing practices in their habitat, specifically gillnet fishing for the totoaba fish, whose swim bladder is highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Gillnets inadvertently capture and drown vaquitas, a phenomenon known as bycatch. Despite legal protections and the banning of gillnets in their habitat, enforcement is lax, and illegal fishing continues unabated.

C. Conservation efforts for the vaquita have been numerous but challenging to implement effectively. These efforts include the establishment of a zero-tolerance zone where no fishing is allowed, and the deployment of the Mexican Navy to enforce fishing regulations. Additionally, conservationists have attempted to use acoustic monitoring to track the vaquita's presence and adjust protective measures accordingly.

D. Despite these initiatives, the future of the vaquita remains uncertain. The species' survival hinges not only on stringent law enforcement but also on broader international cooperation and support. The plight of the vaquita serves as a critical reminder of the impacts of human activities on marine biodiversity and the urgent need for concerted global conservation actions.

E. Not given in any of the above paragraphs.

Decide which paragraph, A to D, has the information given in each statement below. Select E if the information is not given in any of the paragraphs.

-1. The vaquita is recognized by its distinctive dark rings around the eyes and lips.
-2. Less than 20 vaquitas are estimated to remain in the wild today.
-3. The illegal fishing of totoaba fish is a major threat to the survival of the vaquita.
-4. Gillnets, used to catch totoaba, often result in the accidental capture of vaquitas.
-5. Enforcement of the ban on gillnets has been weak, allowing illegal fishing practices to persist.
-6. A designated zero-tolerance zone prohibits all fishing activities to protect the vaquita.
-7. The Mexican Navy has been deployed to help enforce fishing regulations in the vaquita's habitat.
-8. International cooperation is deemed crucial for the effective conservation of the vaquita.
-9. Recent conservation proposals include captive breeding programs to help increase the vaquita population.
