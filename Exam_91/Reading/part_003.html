Hi Jennie,

I’m sorry for taking so long to reply to your letter. As you know, I’ve been very busy this winter with Henry’s college graduation and my parents’ visit from Spain. The graduation ceremony was incredible! I’m sorry you guys couldn’t make it, but we understand it’s a bit of a drive from Winnipeg. You were sincerely missed. They left yesterday for Montreal, and will catch a plane back from there in four days.

Just when we thought things would get more calm, Henry shared the news that he will take a gap year in Australia! He leaves in a few weeks already! And for the next year, he was invited to work at a top advertising agency that specializes in rebranding big companies in Sydney. Needless to say Henry is thrilled about it. The agency has been a big fish in the industry since 2010, and it’s a great starter point into his marketer career, not to mention the amazing opportunity to live in a new place with a new culture. He’s a little worried about the communication, since Australians are known for a very rough accent, but the agency assured him they’ll help him in what he needs.

Henry is overjoyed, but Ron and I are having a tougher time with it. On one hand, we think he’s so young to be so far away, on the other hand, he’s already a grown man. But we all know it’s for the best. Just last week Ron and I were talking about that trip all of us took to Six Flags when the kids were little, do you remember that? It’s been over 15 years! Time flies, doesn’t it? Well, get ready, Alisson is next!

Talk soon,

Love,

Cheryl
